Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

FamilyHub is a private, self-hosted application run by one household for its own use. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what happens to data when the app connects to Google.

What the app accesses

If the owner chooses to connect a Google account, the app requests access to Google Calendar (the calendar scope) so it can read, create, edit and delete calendar events on the owner's behalf. It also reads the account's email address to identify the connection. It does not access Gmail, Drive, Contacts or any other Google service.

How the data is used

Calendar data is used only to show and sync events inside the family's own FamilyHub installation. It is not used for advertising, analytics, profiling or training any AI or machine learning model, and it is not sold or shared with anyone.

Where the data is stored

Events and Google sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's own server, in a private database. Sign-in tokens are encrypted at rest. No data is sent to the developer of this website or to any third party other than Google itself.

Google API Services User Data Policy

FamilyHub's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept until the owner removes it. Disconnecting the Google account in the app deletes the stored tokens and synced events. Access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
