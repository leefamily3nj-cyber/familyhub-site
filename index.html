FamilyHub

FamilyHub is a private, self-hosted family organizer used by a single household. It keeps the family's calendar, chores, shopping lists and meal plans on a home server.

Google Calendar

The app can optionally connect to the owner's own Google Calendar so events appear in the family calendar and stay in sync. It is not a public service, and it is only used by the family that runs it.
